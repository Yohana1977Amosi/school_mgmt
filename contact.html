Quick answers

A few helpful notes.

Who can use the school management system?

Access depends on the role assigned by the school. If you need an account or help with access, contact your school office.

How do I get help with my account?

Use the password recovery option on the login page, or send the school office a message using the form above.

Is the contact form ready to receive messages?

Messages are saved in the school system database. A staff member can review them from the protected Messages page in the dashboard.
